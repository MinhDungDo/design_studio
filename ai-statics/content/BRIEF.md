# Creative Brief

> This file is injected **verbatim** into every Higgsfield ad-generation prompt,
> alongside the user's offer and the awareness-stage directive. It is the brand +
> ICP context the DTC Ads engine uses to write the ad. Keep it tight and concrete —
> everything here lands in the prompt. Do NOT put tooling/command notes here (those
> live in README.md); anything in this file becomes creative direction.

- **Name:** <Brand name>

## The ICP (one ideal customer)
Every ad in a run targets THIS one person at a different awareness stage.
- **Who they are:** <age, role, lifestyle, context>
- **Core pain:** <the problem they feel>
- **Desire / outcome:** <what they actually want>
- **Objections:** <price, trust, effort, skepticism — what holds them back>
- **Where they see ads:** <Instagram feed, stories, TikTok, etc.>

- **Look & feel:** <photographic style, lighting, palette, mood>

- **Avoid:** <clutter, off-brand colors, distorting the product, fake-looking composites>
